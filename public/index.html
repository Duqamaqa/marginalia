How to use Marginalia

  1. Enter an English word.

    A Russian meaning and English example from the same dictionary meaning appear when available. You can edit either field.

  2. Tap Save word.

    The word joins your list and the input clears for the next one. If a word isn’t found, enter your own translation or example to enable Save. Already saved words are flagged automatically.

  3. Tap Export words.

    Choose how many words and your study app. Download a PDF for Knowt’s file uploader, or copy text for Quizlet’s website. The export screen shows each step.

Words are stored on this device. Use More → Back up words to keep a copy or move them to another device. Saved words work offline; online lookup services receive the words you look up.

Add to your iPhone

  1. Open the website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

    Enable “Open as Web App” if shown, then tap Add.

Use the published HTTPS website. Before switching from Safari to the installed app, back up your words and restore them in the app.
