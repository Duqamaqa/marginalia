YOUR NEW READING RITUAL

A margin for your mind.

  1. Meet a word.

    Type it while you read. Russian translation and an English dictionary example appear as available.

  2. Keep the meaning.

    Choose both, translation, or example. Edit for the meaning in your book, then save. The next word gets a fresh page.

  3. Make it stick.

    Export as many words as you want and import them into Quizlet or Knowt.

Your collection stays in this browser on this device. Use Back up to keep a copy or move it. Online lookups use MyMemory and two dictionary providers (dictionaryapi.dev and freedictionaryapi.com); these services receive the words you look up and may have limits. Saved words and manual entries work offline.
ALWAYS WITHIN REACH

On your iPhone, open this website in Safari.

  1. Tap Share

    The square with the upward arrow in Safari’s menu.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Choose “Open as Web App” if shown, then tap Add.

  3. Settle into your reading ritual

    Open Marginalia from its icon for a focused, app-like experience.
